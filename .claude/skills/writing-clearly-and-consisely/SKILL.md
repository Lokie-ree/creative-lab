# Writing Clearly and Concisely

Strunk-style rules for clarity and impact. Apply to anything a human reads.

## Core Rules (Quick Apply)

| Rule | Apply |
|------|--------|
| **Omit needless words** | Cut every word that doesn't earn its place. |
| **Active voice** | "The module validates" not "The input is validated by the module." |
| **Positive form** | State what is, not what isn't, when it's clearer. |
| **Definite, specific language** | Concrete over vague. |
| **Emphatic words at end** | Put the punch at the sentence end. |
| **Related words together** | Keep modifiers next to what they modify. |
